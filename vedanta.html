About
Vedanta Spark is a joint initiative by FORGE Accelerator and Vedanta that focuses on driving innovation and transformation. The main goal of Vedanta Spark is to shape sustainability for a better future. Vedanta Spark is a global corporate innovation, accelerator & ventures program aiming to enable startups that leverage transformative & sustainable technologies to create large-scale impact in partnership with the group companies of Vedanta - the diversified energy & resources conglomerate with operations in 3 continents.

Objectives of the website
The main objective of this redesign was to revamp the existing website for Vedanta Spark in order to better support the goals of the 2022 program. The focus was on creating a more effective and user-friendly website to meet the needs of the 2022 program.
Clear & Comprehensive:
The goal of this website was to present a clear and comprehensive overview of Vedanta Spark, which was lacking from the current site
Streamline User Experience:
Our goal with the redesign was to streamline the user experience and provide only relevant information that would help users make decisions and convert easily.
Maximise Conversion
By simplifying the user flow and focusing on conversion, we hope to maximize the number of conversions on the website.
Scalability:
Scalability is an important aspect of web design and development, as it allows the website to accommodate increased traffic and usage without negatively impacting performance.
Building trust & credibility
Empower potential customers and clients with a comprehensive and current insight into organization.
