About
inFeedo helps elevate employee experience using an AI chatbot called Amber, which chats with a company's employees and proactively finds those who are disengaged, unhappy, or likely to leave. It helps CHROs find gaps in their culture, managers and remote employee experience. Backed by Y Combinator and Bling Capital, inFeedo is one of Asia's leading employee experience platforms that help engage employees and predict attrition. Spanning 60 countries, their customers include MNCs like Samsung, Xiaomi and Lenovo, conglomerates like TATA and Godrej, and unicorns like Paytm and OYO.

Challenges faced by old website
inFeedo, a digital-first brand, engages its audience primarily through its website, social media, emails, and ad campaigns. Catering to HR leaders, CHROs, and HR managers in companies with 500+ employees, the brand encountered challenges in its web presence.
The existing website struggled to articulate key information, hindering potential customers from grasping the true value of inFeedo's innovative solutions
User Engagement:
Low sign-up rates posed a significant challenge, hindered by user engagement and exploration issues.
Navigation
Navigation problems hindered visitors in finding and absorbing crucial information on inFeedo's site.
Visuals:
Website's outdated look demanded a crucial visual upgrade for sustained competitiveness.
Content Structure
The content layout lacked optimization, causing a disconnect in audience understanding.
Value Clarity:
Unclear messaging posed a challenge as potential customers struggled to grasp inFeedo's true value
Scalability
Sparse educational materials restricted self-informative opportunities, hindering understanding of inFeedo's solution benefits
Solution Strategies
Our team aimed to gain a deeper understanding of the company, its offerings, and the problems faced by the website to ensure the redesign was effective and met the needs of both the company and the user. Through both qualitative and quantitative methods, we gathered key information about the company and the website that helped inform the design process.
01
Project Research & Discovery
UX Audit of Website
Heuristic evaluation revealed visual, content, and usability issues, prompting the need for improvements. Key areas include simplifying information structure and redesigning content architecture for enhanced user experience.
Data Analytics
Analytics from platforms like Google Analytics and Hotjar offered insights on demographics, pageviews, conversion rates, user funnels, and more. Specifics included session durations, scroll/click heatmaps, and device/browser shares among users.
Product & Sales Walkthrough
The design team received a walkthrough of inFeedo's product offerings, informing a strategic design alignment. This insight guided our design to effectively support inFeedo's business objectives and sales process.
User Interviews
Stakeholder interviews, from C-level executives to customers, unveiled insights into the website's strengths, weaknesses, and goals. Diverse perspectives helped us comprehend business objectives and user goals for the website more comprehensively.
02
UX Revitalization: Streamlining Sitemap for Seamless Exploration
Leveraging insights from research and competitor analysis, we initiated a UX overhaul. This included a redesigned sitemap, new product pages, and reorganized content for improved user flows
03
A Visual Language with a fresh face
We explored different design directions through references from competitors, similar players in other industries, and unrelated but visually useful sources on the internet. Refining these references provided a visual moodboard to begin designing based on.
